Recommended structure

Places hold data. Lenses change the view.

The blueprint made Engine and Field manual into destinations. That forces people to leave the market to understand it. Here, a place is defined by where its data comes from, and every place offers the same lenses on the selected object.

PLACES · ONE DATA SOURCE EACH

Lessons

  • Recorded evidence from generated-only runs
  • May show individual scripted orders
  • Home object: cork board

Public market

  • Live /v1/public stream of the demo
  • Aggregate levels and sanitized trades only
  • Home object: the board across the street

My run

  • Your isolated run and private stream
  • Invite required; opens only on click
  • Home object: the door with a key tag

Direction A · recommended

Lamplight Workshop

A small exchange workshop at dusk, drawn in front elevation. The room's materials carry the concepts: the journal is a ledger, a queue is a row of pinned index cards, schematics are blueprint sheets, public data is on a board across the street, and your own run is behind your own door.

Direction B · alternative

Night Signal Room

A compact isometric control room at night, shown as a floating diorama. Rain on the window, phosphor monitors, one warm lamp. The lesson becomes an illuminated schematic table.

Asset plan · Direction A

Palette, scale and production

Thirty-two colors in ramps. BUY is blue with ▲, SELL is orange with ▼; alert red appears only with a hazard pattern or icon; uncertain is violet and always dotted.

Grid and perspective

  • World: 320 × 180, integer 4× at 1280 × 720 and 1440 × 900
  • UI: 2× grid; icons 10–16 art px
  • Front elevation; 2–4 px top faces; 16 px tiles
  • Never mix layers inside one object; text is never bitmap-scaled

Light

  • Warm key from the desk lamp, cool fill from the window
  • Palette-locked relighting with 4 × 4 ordered dither
  • Interactive objects one value step lighter at rest

Type

  • Silkscreen at 16 / 32 px for place names and headings only
  • IBM Plex Sans for body, 15 px minimum
  • IBM Plex Mono with tabular figures for prices, quantities, stamps
  • Self-host later; the C++ static server has no .woff2 MIME type today

Formats and budget

  • Source: .aseprite or .pxo, kept out of the served tree
  • Runtime: 1× PNG sheets + JSON frames
  • Example config caps each static asset at 1 MiB; target under 200 KB per sheet, 1.5 MB first load
Available this pass: Python 3 with Pillow, HTML canvas, the browser pane. Not available: any image-generation tool, Aseprite, ImageMagick, or existing pixel sprites, fonts or sounds in the repository. Every image on this page is drawn procedurally from the palette as a layout and value study. Final scene art should be commissioned or hand-drawn.

Prototype scope

One complete journey: Who trades first?

A static, offline prototype with one labelled sample fixture. It makes no network calls and leaves the C++ server and exchange behavior untouched. Asks: 100.04 × 2; 100.02 × 3 (A, seq 7) then 100.02 × 5 (B, seq 9). A scripted buyer sends BUY 4 @ 100.02 GTC. A fills 3, then B fills 1; B keeps 4.

  1. ArriveOne primary card: Start here. The window shows live, quiet or reconnecting via a developer toggle.HOME
  2. BriefThree sentences. Two sellers, one price, one buyer.LESSONS · RECORDED
  3. PredictSelect order A, order B, or "they split it". Keyboard and list alternatives.MARKET LENS
  4. WatchThe buyer card slides in. Two trade slips form in matching order.MARKET LENS
  5. Why?Outcome sentence first, then the manual opens beside the book at price-time priority.FIELD MANUAL
  6. Look insideSame buyer as a token: Generator, Admission, Sequence 12, Journal, Matching, Results.MACHINE LENS
  7. DebriefNo score. One next lesson, "Try it in my run" with the privacy difference stated, Home.LESSONS
  8. ReturnBack lands on Home in one press, focus on the cork board, progress saved in this browser.HOME

Navigation

  • Prediction within three actions of Home
  • Back exits the lesson in one press; steps use replaceState
  • Reload restores the step and saved prediction
  • Focus returns to the object you left from

Honesty

  • One stamp per data panel; prototype banner on every screen
  • No "your order" in a recorded lesson
  • Machine lens starts at Generator
  • Comparison drawer shows no venue claim until a source is verified

Access

  • Everything operable by keyboard in visual order
  • Visible focus on every surface
  • Reduced motion: fades only, ambient loops stop
  • Single-key shortcuts can be switched off; 4.5:1 text contrast; color never the only signal

Comprehension (5+ people)

  • 4 of 5 explain why A traded first
  • 4 of 5 say the lesson was recorded, not live
  • 4 of 5 reach the manual entry unaided
  • Record time to first prediction and any search for Back
